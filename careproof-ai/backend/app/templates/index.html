How We Eliminate Social Media Crowdfunding Fraud

1. AI Claim Verification

Gemini cross-examines hospital quotations with social media claims, flagging inflated targets, mismatched doctors, or forged dates before a campaign goes live.

Human verifier reviews AI risk score & issues cryptographic proof badge.

2. Direct Hospital Escrow

Funds NEVER touch the creator's or influencer's personal bank account or crypto wallet. Payment gateway routes 100% directly to the verified hospital's billing ledger.

Direct RTGS/ACH to Apollo, Fortis, Tata Memorial Escrows.

3. Overfunding Protection

Viral reels often collect \$200,000 for a \$20,000 surgery. CareProof's target cap automatically disables the donate button the exact second the bill is met!

Surplus policy: Automatic refund or Hospital ICU Pediatric pool.
